Before you open your mouth

Pre-flight — do this ten minutes early

1 Run ./run.sh from the samvedna folder. Wait for “ready: http://localhost:3100”. It will not say ready until the stylesheet actually loads, so if it says ready, the page is fine.
2 Open localhost:3100/demo in one tab. Leave it there — it is your prompt sheet, and each cue signs you into the right role.
3 Click Sign out on the role bar, so you start as a member of the public. The first thing judges should see is the pitch, not somebody's caseload.
4 Zoom the browser to 110–125%. The gate formulas are the point and they are small at 100% on a projector.
5 If anything looks unstyled, the servers went down. ./run.sh again — it takes about 40 seconds.

Q & A

The questions you will actually be asked

“What's your model accuracy?”

“About 0.92 AUROC on our synthetic data — and I'd ask you to ignore that number. The published research gets 0.72 on real records, and we designed for that. The four gates exist because the model is not good enough to be trusted alone, and no accuracy figure would change that.”

“What if the model is wrong?”

“It is, often. That's why a score of 1.000 changes no gate value and no verdict — we have a test that asserts it. And if the model file is missing entirely, the night still runs and still escalates the same people.”

“Won't this stigmatise people?”

Go back to cue 3. “125 people were looked at and deliberately not named. Weak evidence produces no name at all — not a watchlist, not a maybe. And only a welfare officer ever sees an identity.”

“Could a commander misuse it?”

Cue 4 again. “His screen contains no identifier. There is no API that would answer 'who is it'. And ACR, promotion, posting and disciplinary purposes are refused for every role — in the software, with a test.”

“Why would a jawan consent?”

“Because the app gives him something: his own trend, the reasons the officer saw, and one-tap withdrawal that nobody is told about. And his self-assessment is worth a fifth of the evidence gate — it's the only input he controls.”

“Have you tested it on real data?”

Say no, plainly. “No. Everything you've seen runs on realistic synthetic records. Phase 1 of our rollout is shadow mode on one battalion's retrospective data — the gates run, every verdict is recorded, and no alert is issued to anybody — so thresholds get calibrated before a single officer is asked to act.”

“What about voice analysis?”

“That's an add-on we explored after submission, and it's labelled as one. It's tiered so it can corroborate a case and never make one. And it's honest about its limit — a man who hides it well is invisible to voice, which is exactly why the other eight signals carry the case.”

“What's left to build?”

“Three things. Seven of our eight translations need a native speaker — the system refuses consent against them until then. Airflow, Keycloak and Postgres are wired as swappable seams but never run against the real thing. And nothing has touched a real record.”

If something breaks live

Page looks unstyled — the servers dropped. ./run.sh, about 40 seconds. It won't say ready until the stylesheet loads.

A page says “Refused” — that is almost certainly the demo working. Check the role bar at the top: you are signed in as somebody who can't see that screen. Say so out loud; it makes the point for you.

No network at the venue — doesn't matter. Nothing here touches the internet. No cloud, no external API, no fonts fetched from anywhere.

Laptop dies — uv run samvedna cohorts and uv run samvedna case pid-mon-0001 run the whole gate engine in a terminal, formulas and all.

One sentence
